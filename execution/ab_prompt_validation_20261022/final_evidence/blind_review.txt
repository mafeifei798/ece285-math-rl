James writes a comic every other day for 4 years. If there was no leap year, how many comics has he written?
RESPONSE
 To determine how many comics James has written, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of days in 4 years.
2. Determine how many of those days are weekends (since James writes a comic every other day, we need to consider the weekends).
3. Calculate the number of comics written based on the number of weekends.

Let's break it down:

1. There are 365 days in a non-leap year. So, in 4 years, there are \(4 \times 365 = 1460\) days.
2. Since James writes a comic every other day, he writes a comic on every second day. This means he writes a comic on half of the days, plus an additional comic if the number of days is odd. Since 1460 is even, he writes a comic on exactly half of the days, which is \(1460 / 2 = 730\) comics.

Let's confirm this with Python code.
```python
# Total number of days in 4 years (assuming no leap years)
total_days = 4 * 365

# Since James writes a comic every other day, he writes a comic on half of the days
comics_written = total_days // 2

print(comics_written)
```
```output
730
```
The total number of comics James has written is \(\boxed{730}\).
